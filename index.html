BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Studio policies.
Good work starts here.

A few simple guidelines help us work professionally, respect your time and deliver the best possible sound.

Ask a question ↗
01

Deposits

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required upfront to secure a recording date or to commence beat production and mixing. The remaining balance is due upon project completion.

02

File Delivery

Final approved tracks are delivered digitally in high-quality WAV and MP3 formats. Track stems are available upon request.

03

Revisions

Mixing and mastering services include 2 free rounds of revisions to ensure you are completely satisfied. Additional revisions will be billed at an hourly rate.

04

Mobile Setup

As a mobile studio, we bring professional recording gear directly to your location. Please ensure the designated space is relatively quiet and free of heavy echoes for the best possible vocal and instrument capture.

05

Punctuality

Billing for recording sessions begins exactly at the scheduled booking time. Please be ready at your agreed appointment time.
